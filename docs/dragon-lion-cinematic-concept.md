# 舞龍舞獅介紹 Cinematic 3D Concept

## Structure

- Target page: `pages/dragon-lion-introduction.html`.
- The homepage is not part of this concept.
- The first implementation is hero-only and lives in the `#lion-scrolly` section.
- The sections below the hero keep the existing page content for now.
- WebGL is handled by `assets/js/lion-scrolly.js` using local Three.js modules.

## Animation Plan

- Hero: sculpture sits in a black / dark wine-red museum space with warm rim light.
- Motion: slow camera/object orbit plus subtle mouse parallax.
- Atmosphere: restrained particles and floor glow only.
- Scroll cue: small understated prompt at the bottom, no additional scroll sections in this pass.

## Visual System

- Background: black, dark wine red, near-black floor.
- Materials: black stone, dark red lacquer, aged bronze, brushed dark metal, restrained gold leaf.
- Typography: large Chinese serif titles, small uppercase English labels.
- Layout: spacious, museum-like, editorial; no mascot, flat lion head, poster composition, or boxed card sequence in the cinematic section.
